# Cases to review

Facts the case writers flagged as uncertain or worth a second look. Tick each once someone with medical training has checked it.

## Bones, joints, immune & hormones

- [ ] Polymyositis: biopsy uses the classic CD8⁺ T cells invading non-necrotic fibres; newer classifications question polymyositis as a separate condition.
- [ ] Serum sickness: C3/C4 reference ranges (0.75–1.65 and 0.14–0.54 g/L) vary by lab.
- [ ] MEN1: gastrin 1,450 ng/L (normal <100) and prolactin 4,800 mU/L are illustrative values.
- [ ] MEN2: calcitonin 1,200 ng/L (normal <5) and CEA 85 µg/L are illustrative values.
- [ ] Posterior hip dislocation: "femoral head looks smaller than the other side" on X-ray.
- [ ] Adhesive capsulitis: thickened coracohumeral ligament on ultrasound used as a supporting finding.

## Gut & kidneys

- [ ] Chronic kidney disease: cause set as long-standing hypertension (small kidneys); note hedges SGLT2 inhibitor since eGFR 16 is below the usual start threshold of 20.
- [ ] Dumping syndrome: oral glucose challenge (pulse rise >10/min in first hour = early; low glucose at 1–3 h = late). Standard enough?
- [ ] Biliary atresia: GGT 650 U/L is an illustrative value; clue 6 combines triangular cord sign with HIDA showing no excretion at 24 h.
- [ ] Hirschsprung's disease: normal immunoreactive trypsinogen in clue 5 (to steer away from meconium ileus). Too obscure?
- [ ] Primary sclerosing cholangitis: p-ANCA shown positive (only ~60–80% of cases).
- [ ] Perforated peptic ulcer: free air on erect CXR quoted as ~three quarters (sources say ~70–80%).
- [ ] Membranous nephropathy: rituximab for higher-risk disease (KDIGO); anticoagulation mentioned.
- [ ] Interstitial cystitis: uses AUA ">6 weeks" duration; pentosan polysulfate kept as an option.
- [ ] Acute interstitial nephritis: urine eosinophils left out on purpose (unreliable test).

## Skin & women's health

- [ ] Bullous pemphigoid: DPP-4 inhibitor (sitagliptin) named as trigger; doxycycline given as an option for widespread disease. Accepts "pemphigoid" as an answer.
- [ ] Pemphigus vulgaris: rituximab + prednisolone as first line; anti-desmoglein 3 described as "strongly positive" without a number.
- [ ] Malignant melanoma: sentinel node biopsy threshold given as >1 mm (guidelines also consider from 0.8 mm); LDH 190 U/L called normal.
- [ ] Atopic eczema: total IgE 1,150 kU/L in a 4-year-old is illustrative; clue 6 cites UK Working Party criteria.
- [ ] Seborrhoeic dermatitis: clue 6 is a treatment response (clears on ketoconazole, relapses off it) since there's no definitive test.
- [ ] Vitiligo: Wood's lamp "bright blue-white"; TSH 7.4 with free T4 11 pmol/L as subclinical hypothyroidism.
- [ ] Meigs syndrome: fluid described only as straw-coloured with negative cytology; CA-125 92 U/mL.
- [ ] Postpartum endometritis: clindamycin + gentamicin as classic regimen (local protocols vary).
- [ ] Pelvic inflammatory disease: BASHH regimen (ceftriaxone once, then 14 days doxycycline + metronidazole).
- [ ] Uterine rupture: CTG 70 bpm for 6 minutes and 1.5 L blood at laparotomy are illustrative.

## Infection & blood

- [ ] Congenital rubella: clue 6 is the triad (echo: PDA + pulmonary artery stenosis; hearing test: sensorineural loss) instead of rubella IgM.
- [ ] Bacillus cereus: food culture >10⁵/g and emetic toxin cereulide named in clue 6.
- [ ] Erythema infectiosum: reticulocytes 0.2% in a healthy child (real but mild dip). Keep?
- [ ] Giant cell tumour of bone: biopsy clue includes H3.3 G34W mutation (~90%). Too niche?
- [ ] Waldenström: serum viscosity "4.6 (normal 1.4–1.8)" is relative, no unit.
- [ ] Essential thrombocythaemia: platelets 920 ×10⁹/L; ~half JAK2+; aspirin + hydroxycarbamide if >60 or prior clot.
- [ ] Leprosy: WHO 2018 three-drug MDT for both forms (6 months PB, 12 months MB).
- [ ] AML: gum swelling left out (monocytic subtype) to fit the MPO-positive marrow.
- [ ] Haemophilia A: plain "haemophilia" not accepted as an answer.

## Heart & lungs

- [ ] Traumatic aortic rupture: femoral pulses weaker than radials (recognised but uncommon sign).
- [ ] Cardiac contusion: hs-troponin I 1,240 ng/L illustrative; normal ECG + troponin makes significant injury unlikely (EAST).
- [ ] Paroxysmal AF: CHA₂DS₂-VASc 3 (age 68, hypertension, female) → offer a DOAC.
- [ ] Small cell lung cancer: SIADH sodium correction ≤10 mmol/L per 24 h (some sources say 8).
- [ ] Pancoast tumour: biopsy shows squamous cell carcinoma (adenocarcinoma also common).
- [ ] Neonatal RDS: gas shows only pH 7.21 and pCO₂ 8.4 kPa.
- [ ] Vasovagal syncope: clue 6 is a tilt-table test, though diagnosis is usually clinical (teaching note says so).
- [ ] Aliases: "post-thrombotic syndrome" accepted for chronic venous insufficiency; "claudication" accepted for peripheral arterial disease.

## Brain, eyes & ears, mind

- [ ] Lambert-Eaton: EMG increment after exercise 150% (usual cut-off >100%; some labs use 60%).
- [ ] Multiple system atrophy: 'hot cross bun' sign (more typical of cerebellar type) used in a mixed parkinsonian/cerebellar case.
- [ ] Focal impaired awareness seizure: febrile status → hippocampal sclerosis is an association; lamotrigine/levetiracetam first line (NICE 2022).
- [ ] Huntington's: normal ≤26 CAG repeats, full penetrance ≥40.
- [ ] Pseudomonas keratitis: clue 6 describes the culture (oxidase+, non-lactose fermenter, blue-green pigment, grape smell) instead of naming it.
- [ ] CMV retinitis: clue 6 is fundus appearance only (no PCR).
- [ ] Amphetamine toxicity: drug described as flatmate's "70 mg long-acting prodrug ADHD capsules".
- [ ] Akathisia: settling within hours of propranolol used as supporting evidence.
- [ ] Factitious disorder: self-injected insulin (insulin 520 pmol/L, C-peptide undetectable, sulfonylurea screen negative).
- [ ] Illustrative scores/values: PCL-5 56 (PTSD), PHQ-9 21 (depression), blood alcohol 15 mmol/L (borderline PD), Delhi 41 °C in June (heat stroke).

## Level gap-fillers (18 cases, written 2026-11-03)

- [ ] Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency: serum level 0.3 g/L for PiZZ (normal about 1.0–2.0 g/L).
- [ ] ABPA: total IgE 2,400 kU/L; itraconazole alongside steroids.
- [ ] Sickle cell crisis: HPLC HbS 88%, HbF 9%; patient from western Odisha.
- [ ] Aluminium phosphide: silver nitrate paper blackening over gastric aspirate as the key finding; hypomagnesaemia and hypoglycaemia in labs.
- [ ] Cyanide poisoning: lactate >8–10 mmol/L threshold in smoke inhalation; COHb 9% alongside.
- [ ] Methanol poisoning: osmolal gap 38 mOsm/kg with lactate 3.0 mmol/L.
- [ ] Amniotic fluid embolism: clue 6 is a dilated right ventricle on bedside echo (supportive, not diagnostic).
- [ ] Acute otitis media: amoxicillin criteria (under 2 with both ears, discharge, or not improving) follow NICE.
- [ ] Difficulty levels for all 180 cases were set by judgment (Easy = common/classic, Hard = rare/subtle); worth a skim.
